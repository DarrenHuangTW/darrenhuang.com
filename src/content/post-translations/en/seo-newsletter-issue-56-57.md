<h2 id="toc-5">[New knowledge] New webpage experience indicator Interaction to Next Paint (INP)</h2>
<p><a href="./core-web-vitals-lcp-fid-cls.html">Web Vitals
In</a>, FID, which represents "interactivity", has two obvious shortcomings. First, it only calculates the delay of one interaction, and there may be multiple interactions with the web page during the entire web browsing process; second, it only calculates the delay time from the user's command to the browser "starting" processing, without taking into account how long the processing takes.</p>
<p>As can be seen in the picture below, a<strong>Interaction delay</strong>consists of three parts, (1.)<code>Input delay</code>Delay from user instruction to start of processing, (2.)<code>Processing Time</code>Total processing time, (3.)<code>Presentation Delay</code>Delay from completion of processing to presentation on screen.</p>
<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large is-resized"><img alt="Illustration for SEO e-Newsletter Issue 56~57 – 2022.05.01~2022.05.31" class="wp-image-3611" decoding="async" height="200" loading="lazy" src="./wp-content/uploads/2022/05/INP%E7%A4%BA%E6%84%8F%E5%9C%96-1024x318.png" width="644"/><figcaption>An interactive delay consists of three parts. Originally, FID only recorded the first part.</figcaption></figure>
<p>And INP takes all interactions during the browsing process of the entire web page<strong>Interaction delay</strong>The largest score as INP. (Note: If a web page view exceeds 50 interactions, the calculation method is:<a href="https://web.dev/inp/#:~:text=INP%20candidate%20by%20number%20of%20page%20interactions.">slightly different</a>。）</p>
<p>Interaction to Next Paint (INP) was unveiled at this year's Google I/O. It is still in the experimental stage, but it aims to solve the above two problems. The development team also said that it hopes to replace FID in the future and become the standard for measuring "interactivity".</p>
<p>Finally, the team is currently working hard to integrate the score into various common tools. If you want to take a first look at the INP score of the website, you can go to CrUX,<a href="https://pagespeed.web.dev/">PageSpeed Insights</a>, or
Seen in<a href="https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/web-vitals/ahfhijdlegdabablpippeagghigmibma">Web Vital Extension</a>.</p>

<h2 id="toc-10">[Abstract] About Sitemap + John’s story about joining Google</h2>

<p>Sitemap came out around 2005, was promoted by Google, and became a standard on the Internet in 2006. At the time, John Mueller was running his own software company. Seeing this new thing, he decided to write a program that allowed everyone to send crawlers to crawl websites and generate sitemaps. As a result, he was invited for an interview by Google and subsequently joined Google.</p>
<p>John said that at that time, sitemap was a new thing, but what was even more eye-opening was the action of sending homemade crawlers to crawl the website. Many people discovered some strange URLs and technical problems on the website.</p>
