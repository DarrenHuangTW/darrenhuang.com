<h2 id="toc-8">[Good article] Three click signals - First, Long, and Last Click</h2>
<p>The importance of click-through rate will be mentioned in introductory SEO courses. This Moz article further analyzes the three forms of clicks we should pursue:</p>
<ul><li>First Click: The web page appears prominently in the search results, attracting users to click</li><li>Long Click: After entering the site, regardless of the content or website experience, it must be able to meet the needs of users.</li><li>Last Click: The information on the web page is sufficient to meet the user's needs without having to go back to the search results page to read a competitor's web page</li></ul>

<h2 id="toc-10">[Abstract] Search Podcast - serving, hreflang, and more</h2>
<p>The previous episodes talked about crawling and indexing. In this episode, Gary wants to talk about "serving". The familiar ranking is only a part of serving. Gary said that serving has two directions, top-down &amp; bottom-up.</p>
<figure class="embed embed--youtube"><div class="embed__frame"><iframe allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" allowfullscreen="" loading="lazy" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/jc7qyMgbraE" title="YouTube video player"></iframe></div><figcaption><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jc7qyMgbraE" rel="noopener noreferrer">View original video on YouTube</a></figcaption></figure>
<p><strong>top down</strong><br/>This means that after Google receives a search term (query), it will go through some processing. Several major steps include<strong>1.)</strong>Parsing and understanding search terms (parsing and understanding), the understanding part includes the processing of synonyms;<strong>2.)</strong>It will then be sent to a router, which will distribute the parsed search terms down to different indexes, including pictures, videos, news... and other different indexes;<strong>3.)</strong>In the index library stage, according to<a class="rank-math-link" href="./seo-newsletter-issue-27.html#toc-8">Introduction to previous episodes</a>Use some of the previous methods (ex: posting list) to filter out relevant results and send them up.</p>
<p><strong>bottom up<br/></strong>After the index library gives the results, it is<strong>1.)</strong>The ranking system comes into play, which is the well-known hundreds of ranking factors, to rank the text given by the index database;<strong>2.)</strong>Then go back up to the router and wait for and collect the results returned by other index libraries.<sup>*</sup>；<strong>3)</strong>Going up further, you will come into contact with the advertising system, and the mechanism there will determine the appearance of those advertisements;<strong>4.)</strong>Then the data is templated and converted into HTML form, and finally the search results page seen by the user.</p>
<p><sup>*</sup>Each index database has a time limit. If the router does not get a response from the index database within the time limit, the final search results will not include this thing. For example, if the index database that manages maps does not return results within the time limit, the final search results for the search term will not have a map (Map Pack) slot. In addition, this process will also involve a lot of caching, so not every search must run through all processes.</p>
