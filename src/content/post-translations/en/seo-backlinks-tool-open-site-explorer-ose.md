<div style="text-align:justify">Backlink is also called Inbound Link (it seems to be called "backlink" in Chinese). Since the blog was launched, there have been two articles mentioning its importance: How Search Engines determine the ranking of the website and Search Engine Calculation of Google Penguin. It is certainly beneficial to understand this concept, but if you are doing SEO analysis for your own company or want to go deeper, you need a tool to help you analyze which websites link to you, who they are, whether they are good links or bad links...etc. This article will introduce a tool developed by a company I like very much, Moz:<a href="https://moz.com/researchtools/ose/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Open Site Explorer</a>!</div>
<h3>Introducing OSE:</h3>
<div>
<div style="text-align:justify">OSE allows users to analyze:<b>How many backlinks are there for your company (or your opponent’s website)? Which websites these backlinks come from? Is the authority of these websites high or low? What is the anchor text of the link?</b>...something like that. The method of use is also very simple. Just put the URL in and let it run and a bunch of data will pop up.</div>
</div>
<div>
<div style="text-align:justify"></div>
</div>

The paid version allows you to see complete information. If you are interested in doing more research, you can register and get a one-month free trial.
