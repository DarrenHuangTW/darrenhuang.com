<h2 id="toc-4">[Case] Clear unnecessary pages (Content Pruning)</h2>
<p>There is an optimization project in SEO called content pruning, which is to delete pages with very low value to make the overall website cleaner, save crawling budget, and reduce the competition between website content. This is usually an operation required by large websites.</p>
<p>I attended a conference a while ago and heard this case analysis. The website deleted 130 million web pages + optimized internal links, and finally had a positive SEO impact. The result is not surprising, but I think this process and ideas, as well as some learnings from it, are very suitable for reference by people who manage large websites!</p>

<h2>[Technology] Cloudflare supports 103 Early Hints</h2>
<p>Most people should have heard of server status codes starting with 2xx, 3xx, 4xx, and 5xx, but those starting with 1 are relatively rare. It was only after reading this article that I became aware of 103 Early Hints, a new technology that can speed up the website.</p>
<p>The advantage of 103 is that it can save the time for the server to prepare a complete response (response). For example, when the browser sends a web page request (request), it must wait until the server responds before it can see the content in the HTML and request the required resources from the server. And 103 is that after the server receives the request, it directly sends back the resources that will be used by the page, and then completely sends the requested web page back to the client. In this case, the server is basically saying "<em>I need to prepare the webpage you requested, but this CSS and this JS will be used later, so accept them first!</em>」</p>
<div class="wp-block-image"><figure class="aligncenter size-large is-resized"><img alt="Illustration for SEO e-Newsletter Issue 41 – 2021.09.16~2021.09.30" class="wp-image-3020" decoding="async" height="279" loading="lazy" src="./wp-content/uploads/2021/09/103-Early-Hints-%E7%8B%80%E6%85%8B%E7%A2%BC-1024x506.png" width="565"/><figcaption>With Early Hints enabled, Cloudflare saves "server think time"</figcaption></figure></div>
<p>If your website uses Cloudflare's CDN, even if you have a free account, you can apply to join 103's beta test. In addition, Cloudflare also has many good features that can help the website speed up. I recommend everyone to try it.</p>

<h2 id="toc-5">[Experiment] The SEO impact of adding internal links at the footer of the homepage</h2>
<p>SearchPilot tested increasing the number of links from 30 to 100 in the footer of the home page, and placing more links to other pages on the website. Result: Newly added pages at the footer have a positive impact on both desktop and mobile devices.</p>

<p>This definitely doesn’t mean you should start cramming pages at the end of the page. Like I said before, this type of testing is best viewed with a grain of salt and tested on your own.</p>

<h2 id="toc-6">[Good article] How to prepare an SEO report for company executives</h2>
<p>Tom Critchlow is a senior SEO senior that I admire very much. he has a<a href="https://seomba.substack.com/">newsletter</a>, not talking about SEO skills and concepts, but focusing on how SEO can gain better trust and reputation within the company. Those who have worked in inhouse may be more familiar with this. The indicators that SEO cares about and the indicators that the company leadership cares about are often different, which makes it easy to talk the same thing when reporting. To successfully implement the project, it is inevitable to obtain the support of the leadership.</p>
<div class="wp-block-image"><figure class="aligncenter size-large is-resized"><img alt="Illustration for SEO e-Newsletter Issue 41 – 2021.09.16~2021.09.30" class="wp-image-3018" decoding="async" height="389" loading="lazy" src="./wp-content/uploads/2021/09/SEO%E5%A0%B1%E5%91%8A-1024x771.png" width="517"/><figcaption>The reality of reporting to company executives</figcaption></figure></div>
<p>Tom’s recent article “<em><a href="https://seomba.substack.com/p/your-boss-cares-about-reporting-more">Your boss cares about reporting more than you do</a></em>”, I was very impressed after reading it. I will briefly share two key points that struck me, but I would also recommend everyone to read the full article.</p>
<p><strong>Don’t use SEO metrics:</strong>SEO reports often contain some numbers that only SEO people can appreciate, such as "<em>Website authority (domain authority)</em>」、「<em>Number of top three keywords</em>」、「<em>Number of keywords appearing in featured snippets</em>"...etc. Focusing on the company's revenue, often a report only contains traffic and revenue, which is quite clear and intuitive.</p>
<p><strong>Don’t differentiate based on web page categories alone</strong>: SEO reports commonly use web page types to distinguish, such as "<em>front page</em>」、「<em>Category page</em>」、「<em>product page</em>"...etc., but for senior management, they may be more concerned about the differences between tier 1, tier 2, and tier 3 sellers, the growth of new and old customer groups, and the comparison of market performance in different regions. So pay more attention to how leadership categorizes the company's performance when reporting on it, or sit down with senior executives to have a conversation about what kind of reports they want to see.</p>

<h2 id="toc-10">[Video] Will the theme of the website affect SEO?</h2>
<p>The answer is "yes" - the background theme of the website, in addition to the visual presentation such as color and fonts, also usually affects more technical aspects such as links, website structure, and loading speed.</p>
<p>Therefore, when choosing a theme for a WordPress website, it is not just beautiful, because usually beautiful websites tend to have a lot of complex codes mixed together, resulting in a speed disadvantage. A better method is to build a test website, and then do some SEO analysis to confirm that the new theme does not have too many problems and that the customized areas can be customized, and then implement the new theme to the main website.</p>
