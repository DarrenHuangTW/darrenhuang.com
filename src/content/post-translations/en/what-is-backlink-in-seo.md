<h2><strong>What are Backlinks</strong>(backlink)?</h2>
<p><strong>Backlink in Chinese is "reverse link", which refers to "links from other websites to your website"</strong>is sometimes called "Inbound Links" in English. When I mention relevant information or quote other people's articles when writing articles, I will add a hyperlink to the other party's website so that interested readers can visit the website on their own. Such a hyperlink is a backlink for those websites.</p>
<p>Sometimes you will see different terms. These terms do not have absolute definitions, so if you have any doubts during communication, you may wish to ask to confirm the understanding of both parties.</p>
<ul><li><strong>External Link or Outbound Link</strong>: Links from your website to other people’s websites</li><li><strong>Internal Link</strong>: Link to the same website</li></ul>

<h2>A brief discussion on PageRank</h2>
<p><strong>PageRank is a score calculated by search engines for each web page through links between web pages.</strong>. The academic paper on PageRank was first published by the two founders of Google. They later applied it to online search and created Google, which everyone knows. Thanks to this technology, Google can give better results than other search engines and gain a leading position among search engines.
The calculation method of</p>
<p>PageRank is very complicated. You can think of this paragraph as a Good to Know and explain it with a simplified and simplified diagram below:</p>

<ul><li><strong>page A is 0.4</strong>, because page A has a link from page C, and page C’s PageRank is 0.4 and has only one external link.
The PageRank of</li><li><strong>page B is 0.2</strong>, because page B has a link from page A, and page A’s PageRank is 0.4 but has two external links, so each link gets 0.2 (0.4/2)
The PageRank of</li><li><strong>page C is 0.</strong>4. Because page C has a link from page A and a link from page B, getting page A will get 0.2 points (0.4/2) plus the 0.2 points from page B.</li></ul>

<h2>How should webmasters view backlinks with a correct attitude?</h2>
<p>The above three classifications simply distinguish which are good and which are bad backlinks, but sometimes what some SEOs are doing is not so black and white, for example:</p>
<ol><li>The owners of the two websites have communicated in advance. I will write an article mentioning you, and you will write an article mentioning me.</li><li>The restaurant invites food bloggers to dine for free, but they must write an article to promote the restaurant and include a link.</li><li>Offer to write blogs for big websites, even pay them, and then insert a link to your own website in the article.</li></ol>
<p>Not all the motivations for these behaviors are so bad, and not all the quality of such articles is bad, but as a marketer, you must be reminded<strong>Never do Link Building just for SEO and website ranking reasons</strong>, in fact, the word Link "Building" personally feels a bit outlandish, because it means that you care about the Link rather than the real website users.</p>
<p>Many SEO agencies will also use the establishment of backlinks as one of their KPIs. In addition to determining whether the source of the link is "natural", as webmasters, we should also pay attention to whether these links bring real "value" (traffic, brand exposure, etc.).</p>
