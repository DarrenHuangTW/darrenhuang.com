<h2 id="toc-1">Featured snippets may appear in positions other than the first position</h2>
<p>Some netizens have noticed that featured snippets (FS) appear in positions other than the first position. In the past, featured snippets would only appear at the top of the search results, so they were often called "position 0". Web pages that appeared in FS would also appear in the general results below. Now these two generally accepted rules are no longer absolute.</p>

<h2 id="toc-3">Bing gives webmasters more control over content in snippets</h2>
<p>Bing has opened the following three robots meta tags, allowing webmasters to have more control over the style of web pages that appear in Bing search results:</p>
<ul><li><strong>max-snippet:[number]</strong>— Limit the length of text that appears</li><li><strong>max-image-preview:[value</strong>] — Limit the size at which images appear</li><li><strong>max-video-preview:[number]</strong>— Limit the length of the video (in seconds)</li></ul>
<p>It is worth mentioning that Google also started supporting these tags last year.</p>
<p>link:<a href="https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/april-2020/Announcing-new-options-for-webmasters-to-control-their-snippets-at-Bing">Bing Announcement</a> / <a href="https://webmasters.googleblog.com/2019/09/more-controls-on-search.html">Google Announcement</a></p>
