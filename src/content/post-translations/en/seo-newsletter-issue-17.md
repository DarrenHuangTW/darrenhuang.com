<h2 id="toc-8">Googlebot will start crawling with HTTP/2</h2>
<p><strong>Starting in November 2020, Googlebot will crawl some websites using HTTP/2 (h2).</strong></p>
<p>Fetching with HTTP/2 can be more efficient and save Google's resources. Even if the server supports it, the webmaster has no way to force Googlebot to crawl with h2. It is up to Google to decide whether to upgrade from HTTP/1.1 to HTTP/2.</p>
<p>When Google decides to crawl your website with h2, the webmaster will receive a notification in GSC. In addition, you can also use server logs to see how Googlebot crawls.
The main advantage of</p>
<p><strong>website supporting h2 is that it is faster and saves resources, and it will not affect the indexing and ranking of the website.</strong></p>
