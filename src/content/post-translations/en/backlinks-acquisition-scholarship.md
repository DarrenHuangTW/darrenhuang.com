<h3>COC Scholarships Links Acquisition Strategy</h3>
<div>This method should have existed in the American SEO industry for a while, but this is the first time COC has implemented it. The scholarship page has been completed by other web designers in the company. I am responsible for finding which schools offer scholarships and contacting them.</div>
<div></div>
<h3>Advantages:</h3>
<div>1. The company provides a fixed amount of money (COC provides $1,000). How many links you can get depends on your personality. The more links, the lower the ROI.<div></div>2. The TLD of these backlinks is ".edu". The Domain Authority of these school institutions is usually very high, and the link juice that comes with it is relatively valuable.</div>
<div>There is an important concept here,<b>The links between .edu and .gov are not inherently more important than .com!</b>, but because these government/educational institutions themselves usually have higher DA, Matt Cutts has said this!</div>
<div>

<div style="text-align:center"></div>3. Shareablility: The news of getting a 1,000 yuan scholarship is worth sharing. Although it is difficult to track, it may attract some secondary links. Generally speaking, scholarships with no restrictions may not be successful, but if they are scholarships specifically for a specific ethnic group (girls/blacks/Latin Americans, etc.), it will easily attract some ethnic-related websites to repost the information.</div>
<div></div>
<div>4. Dwell Time: People who click on the scholarship page usually take the time to read the application conditions, qualifications, requirements, and even look at what the company that provides the scholarship does. The time they stay on the web page may also tell Google that this is an important website.</div>

<h3>How to find schools with external scholarship pages</h3>
</div>
<div>Not every school will have a dedicated page for External Scholarship. There are so many schools in the United States that it is impossible to find out if each other has such a page. <h3>Hindsight/review and improvement</h3>1. When contacting the school, the title of my email was "COC Scholarship Program", so most schools also use this name when posting external scholarship webpages, but a better way would be to use "COC Digital Marketing Scholarship Program".

Firstly, because the company is related to digital marketing, adding these two words can have the effect of co-occurence. Secondly, it allows people who are really looking for scholarships to notice what kind of scholarship this is related to.

2. Design the scholarship to be awarded once a year. Most schools will remove the link because the scholarship has expired, so you should tell them that this is Annually when you first pitch to the school. This will not only prevent the other party from removing the link, but also prevent you from having to work hard again the next year.

3. The sooner you have this program, the better. I have looked at the external scholarships pages of hundreds of schools, and some have not been updated for months or even years. Assuming that a school has not updated its page since 2013, it will be difficult for companies that want to join the competition after 2013 to succeed.

Another benefit is that long-term cooperation will increase the credibility of the company's own scholarship program. If there are testimonials from the winners from 2007 to 2015 on the scholarship event's webpage, the school will also think that it is a company that has made some contributions in this field for a long time.<div></div>
