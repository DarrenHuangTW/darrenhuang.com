<h3 style="text-align:justify">What is HARO?</h3>
<div style="text-align:justify"><a href="http://www.helpareporter.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">HARO</a>The full name is Help A Reporter Out, which is a link between "journalists/bloggers" and "people with professional knowledge/some knowledge of certain fields". It is a Press Request platform. Suppose a reporter wants to write about Google's latest Panda 4.0 update, but he is not an expert in this field and needs help with corrections. Or he has written a report but needs to quote one or two words from experts to make his article more informative. He can use the HARO platform to let others Help a reporter out.</div>

<h3 style="text-align:justify">What are the benefits of HARO?</h3>
<div style="text-align:justify">There is mutual benefit between reporters and companies that need exposure. Journalists who have little connection with experts can still get expert opinions here, and small companies also have an extra chance to be exposed in media newspapers. If I answer a reporter’s question, and he thinks what I said is very constructive and is willing to include it in the article, usually the reporter will say when quoting you, "<a href="./" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">D.H. Digital Marketing Notes</a>Darren of "N*&amp;^@(&amp;$!&amp;." believes that in addition to exposure, you also get a backlink! Such a backlink is very valuable, because</div>
<div style="text-align:justify"></div>
<ol>
<li style="text-align:justify">It is an editorial link. The other party has written an article seriously, and it may be very related to your company's products or services. This website will be reposted on FB, and will be seen by many people, which will increase a lot of traffic.</li>
<li style="text-align:justify">The website of the article usually has a very high page/domain authority. If the reporter represents a major newspaper such as TIME or WSJ, the value of this backlink cannot be matched by any other.</li>
<li style="text-align:justify">Establish a good relationship with the reporter. If the reporter wants to write articles on similar topics in the future, he may come to you directly instead of going through HARO.</li>

<h3 style="text-align:justify">Use IFTTT to find content that’s right for you</h3>
<div>
<div style="text-align:justify">There is a picture before that HARO’s classification is a bit rough. You can use it at this time.<a href="https://ifttt.com/recipes" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">IFTTT</a>This tool uses other methods to notify you when a Query you are interested in appears. This method is very helpful for busy people, because HARO sends several questions from reporters every day, and it takes some time to read them all.</div>
</div>
<div>
<div style="text-align:justify"></div>
</div>
<div>
<div style="text-align:justify">The full name of IFTTT is If This Then That. It is a super powerful tool. After connecting to other platforms or mobile phones, you can create so-called recipes.</div>
</div>
<div>
<ul>
<li style="text-align:justify">You can connect the ESPN website to your mobile phone and set up a<i> </i><b>"IT The Golden State Warriors game is over, THEN mobile phone notifies me of the score"</b></li>
</ul>

<li style="text-align:justify">You can also connect FB and Google Drive and set one<b>"IF you are tagged in someone else's uploaded photo, THEN back up the photo to Google Drive"</b></li>
</ul>
<div>
<div style="text-align:justify"><a href="https://ifttt.com/view_embed_recipe/54681-download-new-facebook-photos-you-re-tagged-in-to-a-google-drive-folder" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank"><img alt="Illustration for SEO | A good platform to increase backlinks-HARO" decoding="async" loading="lazy" src="https://ifttt.com/recipe_embed_img/54681" style="max-width:100%"/></a></div>

<div style="text-align:justify">I will write a complete teaching article when I become more familiar with IFTTT and have time. But the concept is roughly like this. Companies or merchants can connect Gmail and SMS to create a Recipe and say:<b>"IF you receive an email from HARO and it contains SEO or digital marketing, THEN send me a text message to notify me."</b></div>
