<h2 id="toc-2">[Official] Does automatically generating content violate Google policy?</h2>
<p>Machine-produced content is definitely an important aspect of Google and SEO in the next two to three years. John Mueller said in a recent<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yx997SsiYlw&amp;t=1334s">is under official QA</a>replied that the content of the machine is spam and violates Google's policy. <h2 id="toc-4">[Official] Google 2021 Webspam Report</h2>
<p>The Webspam Report is a report that is released in March and April every year. It does not have many action items, but it can review various updates that have occurred over the past year. A new term appears in this report - "SpamBrain", which uses machine learning to combat online spam. SpamBrain was launched in 2018 and continues to be optimized. Compared with the previous year (2020), it caught nearly six times as many spam websites.</p>
<p>In the announcement, Google said that the three key points in 2021 are: (1.) "Launched in July last year,<a href="./seo-newsletter-issue-37.html#toc-1">Link Spam Update</a>”, more effectively identify unnatural links; (2.) Reduce 40% of fraudulent results appearing in searches; (3.) Provide<a href="./seo-newsletter-issue-51.html#toc-8">Personal content removal request</a>to reduce online harassment.</p>
<p>Another interesting point is that Google changed the "<a href="./seo-newsletter-issue-46.html#toc-1">Product review algorithm update</a>” was also included in the report of “webspam”, believing that they have greatly improved the phenomenon that the original search results were full of “only rewritten product descriptions and insincere actual reviews”.</p>

<h2 id="toc-5">[News] Google Merchant “Trusted Store” Badge</h2>
<p>Google Shopping has launched a "shopping experience" scoring mechanism to make merchants that provide a good shopping experience more likely to be noticed by users, including a "Trusted Store" badge, improved rankings in shopping searches, etc.</p>
<p>The calculation of this score is mainly based on four indicators, delivery time, freight, return time, and return cost. Each indicator will be scored as "excellent", "high quality" or "opportunity for improvement". Currently it only applies to merchants on Google Shopping in the United States.</p>
