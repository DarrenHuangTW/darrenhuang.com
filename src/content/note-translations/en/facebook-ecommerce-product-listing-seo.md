This post recommends an article about the optimization of e-commerce product list pages, also known as PLP or category pages.

PLP often encounters the pull between SEO and UX: SEO may want the page to have more text, while the design and product teams want the page to remain clear, fast, and easy to navigate.

A better compromise is to put the content where users really need it when making decisions, such as answering frequently asked questions, explaining classification differences, or providing information that can assist in making choices, rather than stuffing product history and irrelevant paragraphs for search engines.

The original article also shared experiments on deleting category page text and adjusting the URL structure.

This type of testing cannot be considered a risk-free one-size-fits-all answer, and any URL or page content adjustments should be accompanied by monitoring, staged testing, and rollback plans.
