This post summarizes several working methods that SEO workers began to try in the early days of ChatGPT’s popularity.

Case studies include using prompt words to assist technical SEO, organizing content, analyzing keywords and competitors, and using translation tools to lower the threshold for reading English materials.

The common point in these cases is not to treat the model as an automatic text production machine, but to first break the work into steps that can be checked, and then let the model assist in generating drafts, categories, or research directions.

Most of the original links are articles and tool introductions from 2023, and the interface, model capabilities, and service status may have changed.

Therefore, what remains here is an early observation: when AI tools enter SEO work, what really needs to be accumulated is problem definition, judgment and verification capabilities.
